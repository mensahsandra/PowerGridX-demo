# PowerGridX (PGX) - Demo Repository

Welcome to the demo repository for **PowerGridX (PGX)** — a grassroots smart energy initiative building modular infrastructure to tackle electricity theft, unreliable power and poor grid visibility across Ghana and sub-Saharan Africa.

PGX integrates **tamper-resistant smart meters**, **solar-powered smart poles**, **secure mobile top-ups**, **mini-grid readiness**, and **blockchain-based authentication** to reduce losses, empower users and modernize underserved power systems.

---

## 🔍 Repository Overview

This repository contains:

1. **Smart Meter Concept** – Tamper detection, transformer handshake logic, and secure recharge
2. **Smart Pole Sketch** – Solar-powered IoT pole with LoRa, public lighting, and sensors
3. **Passive Smart Node (PSN)** – Low-power relay device for legacy pole upgrades
4. **Mini-Grid Integration** – PGX-ready smart modules for solar or hybrid microgrids
5. **System Architecture** – End-to-end energy flow from generation to user dashboard
6. **UI & USSD Mockups** – Contractor dashboard, mobile app, and recharge logic
7. **Field Insight PDF** – Asset tagging reports & field documentation from ECG/BPS projects
8. **Letters of Intent** – Interest from early supporters, testers, and collaborators

## ⚡ Why Passive Smart Nodes Matter

**PGX PSNs** are small, solar-powered IoT devices installed on existing utility poles. They detect tampering and unauthorized tapping — a common method of electricity theft, by monitoring line activity in real-time and relaying alerts to the PGX Cloud.

PSNs allow for **cost-effective upgrades** of legacy power lines, bridging old infrastructure with modern visibility without full replacement.

---

## ⚡ Why Mini-Grids Matter

PGX is designed to seamlessly integrate with **solar or hybrid mini-grids** that power rural communities, schools and health centers. These modular, decentralized energy systems reduce reliance on national grids and deliver consistent power to off-grid areas.

Using PGX meters, smart poles, and cloud tools:
- Communities can monitor usage transparently
- Utilities or developers can track tampering, performance, and payments
- Field agents can pair meters and report faults via mobile or USSD

Mini-grids are a key part of Africa’s energy future, and PGX strengthens their transparency, accountability and community trust.

## 🌍 Why This Project Matters

Over 600 million people in Africa experience unreliable or no electricity. PGX is building a secure, IoT-enabled smart infrastructure layer that leverages **community-first hardware**, **renewables** and **cloud connectivity** to protect utilities, strengthen access and deliver trust in power systems.
