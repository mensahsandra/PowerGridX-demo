Introduction

PowerGridX is a smart infrastructure initiative addressing energy access across underserved African communities. Using tamper resistant smart meters, renewable smart poles and mobile first systems, we drive down inefficiency, theft, and outages.

Our platform delivers real-time asset visibility, secure contractor tools and accessible recharge options empowering utilities, technicians and residents alike.

The Challenges – Field Insights

(Based on real-world experience from ECG & BPS digitalization project in Ghana)

High Energy Theft & Losses

Over 30% of electricity in Ghana is lost, largely due to theft, illegal connections and unregistered meters. ECG reported 32% total losses in 2024, with a significant portion tied to non-technical (commercial) losses. These leakages severely weaken utility revenues and long-term grid stability.

Source: Energy Commission of Ghana, ACEP

Broken Visibility & Data Gaps

Most meters were not previously linked to their poles or transformers, making tamper detection and fault tracing difficult. ECG only began implementing a GIS-based grid mapping system in 2022. Prior to this, tens of thousands of meters were effectively invisible in utility systems.

Source: MCC/ECG GIS project reports

Widespread Tampering & Ghost Consumption

Unregistered, diverted, and tampered meters are especially common in rural and peri-urban areas. In one audit, over 10,000 illegal meters were discovered in just one region. Ghost billing and fake meter imports continue to drain resources and damage public trust.

Source: ACEP audit, ECG/NEDCo reports

Manual Processes & Inefficiency

Installation and fault resolution still rely heavily on informal contractors, handwritten reports and delayed physical follow-ups. Lack of digital systems reduces accountability and leads to slow response times in both urban and rural deployments.

Customer Frustration & Low Trust

Frequent complaints from customers include unclear billing, delays in fault repairs and no visibility into usage or recharge status. These frustrations fuel illegal connections and reduce payment compliance, further worsening utility losses.

Impact

Enable Digital-First Energy Access in Unreached Zones

PGX is designed to serve peri-urban and rural areas where digital services and infrastructure are often skipped. Our system works with or without high-speed internet, meaning it can empower last-mile users without excluding them.

Make Power Management Local and Traceable

By pairing each meter with a contractor, pole and community dashboard, PGX decentralizes grid visibility, so local workers are accountable, and local users are informed. This flips the trust model from top-down to community-first.

Build the Foundation for Smart Grid Readiness in West Africa

PGX doesn't just fix tampering, it lays the groundwork for a smart energy future: from load forecasting to community-level energy analytics. It's a step toward resilient, self-regulating grids.

Key Components

Smart Meter

Tamper-Resistant, Digitally Secured

  • Embedded tamper sensors
  • Transformer-authenticated pairing
  • LoRaWAN/Satellite-ready
  • USSD + mobile top-up
  • Blockchain monitoring enabled

Smart Pole

A multi-purpose, solar-powered infrastructure node.

  • Solar + battery backup
  • Dual LED public lighting
  • LoRa base node placement
  • Optional: mosquito-repelling tech
  • Blockchain monitoring enabled

Passive Smart Nodes (PSN)

Small, low-cost nodes for real-time grid monitoring.

  • Monitors voltage, current, and line activity
  • Detects illegal connections and faults
  • Sends data via LoRa to the cloud
  • Solar-powered and easy to install

Mini Grid Integration

PGX can integrate with or support mini-grids in off-grid rural areas.

  • Smart poles act as localized energy nodes
  • PGX meters for accurate usage & micropayments
  • USSD/mobile recharge for offline regions
  • Remote monitoring of usage, payments, and faults

PowerGridX Architecture

The full PowerGridX system integrates smart meters, smart poles, PSNs, mini grid, cloud backend and blockchain. Data is securely relayed from meters and poles to the cloud and immutable blockchain, enabling real-time monitoring and secure top-up.

Passive Smart Nodes (PSN) Architecture

Passive Smart Nodes (PSNs) are small solar powered nodes installed on existing poles and power lines. They monitor voltage, current and line activity in real time also, and sends data via LoRa to the cloud.

User Interface

Our mobile-first interface allows users to track usage, receive tamper alerts, top-up credit via USSD or mobile app, and report faults. Accessible and intuitive for everyone.

Contractor Interface

Licensed contractors use the PGX portal to authenticate, view jobs, scan, pair meters and submit geo-tagged, blockchain-logged reports, ensuring full traceability of all actions.

Use Cases & Success Stories

Maame Ama, Landlord

Maame Ama owns a compound house and struggles with tenants not paying their electricity bills on time. With PGX, she can monitor each unit's consumption, receive low-credit alerts and ensure tenants top-up via USSD, preventing unpaid bills and disputes.

Akua, Field Engineer

Akua is a licensed contractor who used to spend hours on manual installation reports. With the PGX app, she scans meters, pairs them with poles instantly and submits geo-tagged, blockchain-logged reports in minutes, improving her efficiency and providing a verifiable work history.

Admin View, Utility Co.

From the PGX dashboard, utility staff can spot unusual activity or tampering in real-time, assign a licensed contractor for maintenance, and audit all actions transparently. This reduces losses and improves grid management.
