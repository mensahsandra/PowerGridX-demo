Introduction

PowerGridX is a smart infrastructure initiative addressing energy access across underserved African communities. Using tamper resistant smart meters, renewable smart poles and mobile first systems, we drive down inefficiency, theft, and outages.

Our platform delivers real-time asset visibility, secure contractor tools and accessible recharge options empowering utilities, technicians and residents alike.

The Challenges – Field Insights

(Based on real-world experience from ECG & BPS digitalization project in Ghana)

High Energy Theft & Losses

Over 40% of power is lost to theft, illegal connections and unregistered meters, crippling utility revenues.

Widespread Tampering & Power Theft

Unregistered meters, illegal connections and ghost billing, especially in peri-urban and rural areas continue to drain resources.

Broken Visibility & Data Gaps

Most meters are not linked to specific transformers or poles, making diagnosis and accountability difficult.

Manual Processes & Inefficiency

Installations and fault reporting rely on informal contractors, handwritten notes and delayed follow-ups.

Customer Frustration

Common complaints include unclear bills, long fault resolution times and no insight into usage or top-ups.

What We're Building

  • Tamper-proof smart meters with keypad + contractor-ID slot
  • Solar-powered smart poles (IoT enabled, public lighting)
  • Passive monitoring nodes to detect tampering
  • Cloud dashboard + blockchain log for real-time trust
  • Mini-grid-ready for off-grid & essential services

Why Now?

  • Traditional meter technology is outdated and easy to tamper with
  • Rising demand for secure, remote management of electricity infrastructure
  • Urgent need to expand access and improve reliability across underserved areas
  • Energy Theft is a Public Cost and a Private Risk: As energy theft and tampering go unchecked, utilities lose revenue and communities lose trust.
    Every bypassed meter weakens the grid and with Ghana's increasing load demand, even small inefficiencies now create wide-scale outages tomorrow.
  • A Demand for Traceable Infrastructure: PGX is aligned with this shift, offering blockchain-secured logs and transparent service records to help meet ESG, SDG and governance benchmarks.

Why It Matters

  • Tamper-resistant meters with transformer-pairing
  • Contractor ID-linked installations
  • Smart poles with passive nodes to detect illegal taps
  • Inclusive USSD & mobile-first recharge access
  • Real-time fault alerts + cloud-integrated logs
  • Audit trail secured by blockchain

Tackling energy theft, grid unreliability and bill transparency with scalable, modular solutions.

Aligned with World Bank Goals: 90% Global Electricity access & 210K+ mini-grids by 2030 (e3g.org)

Impact

  • Enable Digital-First Energy Access in Unreached Zones
    PGX is designed to serve peri-urban and rural areas where digital services and infrastructure are often skipped. Our system works with or without high-speed internet, meaning it can empower last-mile users without excluding them.
  • Make Power Management Local and Traceable
    By pairing each meter with a contractor, pole, and community dashboard, PGX decentralizes grid visibility, so local workers are accountable, and local users are informed. This flips the trust model from top-down to community-first.
  • Build the Foundation for Smart Grid Readiness in West Africa
    PGX doesn't just fix tampering, it lays the groundwork for a smart energy future: from load forecasting to community-level energy analytics. It's a step toward resilient, self-regulating grids.

Mini Grid

PGX can integrate with or support mini grids in off-grid rural areas by:

  • Using smart poles + renewable solar as localized energy nodes
  • Deploying PGX meters for accurate usage & micro payments
  • Enabling USSD/mobile recharge for offline or low connectivity regions
  • Allowing PGX cloud + blockchain to monitor usage, payments and faults remotely

This allows communities to run local generation while maintaining digital energy records and billing transparency.

PowerGridX Architecture

The full PowerGridX system integrates smart meters, smart poles, PSNs, mini grid, cloud backend and blockchain.
Data is securely relayed from meters and poles to the cloud and immutable blockchain, enabling real-time monitoring and secure top-up.

Passive Smart Nodes (PNS) Architecture

Passive Smart Nodes (PSNs) are small solar powered nodes installed on existing poles and power lines.

  • It monitors voltage, current and line activity in real time.
  • Sends data via LoRa to cloud.

User Interface

Offers:

  • Live usage updates (voltage, power, estimated billing)
  • Tamper alerts and outage reports
  • Seamless mobile/USSD top-ups
  • Transaction history + receipt download
  • Smart alerts: when credit is low, faults occur, or service is due

This can be accessed via a mobile app or USSD, ensuring inclusivity even without smartphones.

Contractor Interface

Licensed contractors use the PGX portal to:

  • Authenticate themselves via QR or biometric login
  • View assigned installations or maintenance jobs
  • Scan and pair meters with specific poles/transformers
  • Upload photos, GPS locations, and fault status
  • Submit reports directly to the PGX cloud (for ECG/BPS review)

Every meter action is logged and tied to the contractor's ID to ensure full traceability.
